About

Overview

The Animal AI Incident Observatory is a public, citable record of cases where artificial intelligence systems have directly harmed animals.

Animals are rarely treated as stakeholders in conversations about AI risk, safety, or governance — despite being directly affected by AI systems in areas such as agriculture, autonomous vehicles, wildlife management, and robotics. Without a centralized record, these incidents stay scattered across news archives, institutional reports, and research papers that are hard to find and compare.

How incidents are collected

The observatory pulls from various news sources, public reports, and research papers for instances of AI system impact on non-human animals. Each candidate incident goes through an LLM-assisted review pipeline followed by a human moderator review before publication.

Each incident is classified along several dimensions like animal type, number of animals impacted, location of the incident, etc. Official taxonomy and operational definitions are still in progress.
